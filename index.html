Keep labor & delivery and the newborn nursery open at Watsonville Community Hospital

The Intermediate Intensive Care Nursery (IICN) — where newborns who need extra help are cared for — has been closed since July 10, 2026 because of a shortage of nursing staff.

Watsonville Community Hospital delivers about 31% of all babies born in Santa Cruz County. Many families in the Pajaro Valley worry that labor & delivery could be affected next.

The District Board has not announced a scheduled vote to close labor & delivery. This site is about asking them to keep it open and to fix the IICN staffing problem — not reacting to a vote that has been announced.
